# Privacy Policy — Kurdish Sorani Keyboard

**Kurdish Sorani Keyboard** does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

- No analytics
- No tracking
- No internet connection required or used
- No data shared with third parties

The only data stored locally is a single flag (`hasOnboarded`) in macOS UserDefaults, which records whether the setup window has been shown. This never leaves your device.
